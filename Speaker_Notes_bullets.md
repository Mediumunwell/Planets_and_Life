# Speaker Notes - Bullet Version Final Draft

Target: about 4 minutes.

## Slide 1 - Habitability Is Layered

- Habitability is more than "planet in liquid-water zone."
  - Galactic address: safe enough, but metal-rich enough.
  - Planet conditions: atmosphere, water, gravity, moons, tectonics.
  - Carbonate-silicate cycle = slow climate thermostat.

- Gaia idea:
  - Not mystical "Earth is alive."
  - Biology and planet shape each other.

## Slide 2 - Anthropic Timing as a Filter Question

- Copernican principle: do not assume our world or time is special.
- Anthropic principle: our evidence comes from a history where observers exist.
- Hard steps: origin of life, photosynthesis, eukaryotes, animals, intelligence.
- If those steps are rare, the quiet universe makes sense.
- If they are easy, maybe the dangerous filter is still ahead.

## Slide 3 - The Clocks Need Chemistry

- Top graph:
  - Longer habitable windows give more chances.
  - Red dwarfs should dominate if equally habitable.

- Bottom graph:
  - Earlier abiogenesis helps modestly.
  - Mars / asteroids shift the frame toward early inner solar system.

- Miller-Urey:
  - Small molecules can become amino acids with water, atmosphere, and energy.

- Symbiogenesis:
  - Complexity later needs cooperation becoming inheritance.
  - Origin of life and origin of complex cells are separate hard problems.

## Slide 4 - Origin Clues Memory Board

- Iron-sulfide hot springs:
  - Hot mineral water, wet-dry cycles, catalytic sulfide surfaces.

- Loki's Castle:
  - Deep-sea vent version: mineral chimneys and chemical gradients.

- Korean crater:
  - Hapcheon / Jeokjung-Chogye basin crop.
  - About 42,300 years ago.
  - Rice fields and mountains hiding an impact crater.
  - Azaleas and floating solar panels = lived-in landscape.
  - Crater lakes can be warm, mineral-rich habitats.

- Stromatolites:
  - Shark Bay as the clean visual.
  - Layered microbial structures.
  - Life as a community that builds rocks.

## Slide 5 - Gaia, Twice

- Gaia mission:
  - Astrometry measures tiny sideways star wobbles.
  - Complements transits and Doppler.

- Gaia idea:
  - Life changes atmosphere, chemistry, climate, and habitability.

- Mars as nearby test:
  - Kaolinite = persistent water-rock weathering.
  - Long-chain organics = decane, undecane, dodecane.
  - Wet clay, organics, and rock exchange ask whether the first step was solar-system scale.

- Final turn:
  - We are not central; we are filtered.
  - Which filters are behind us, and which remain?
